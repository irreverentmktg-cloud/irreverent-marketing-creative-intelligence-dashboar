Every week, your competitor's best-performing ad is sitting in the Meta Ad Library. Public. Free. Running because it's paying for itself.

And most marketers are scrolling past it.

**Steal the Signal** is the method for reading it.

---

### The core insight

Meta doesn't show you spend data, CTR, or ROAS for competitor ads.

What it shows you — and what almost nobody uses as a signal — is how long an ad has been running.

An ad that's been active for 45 days cleared every kill threshold the media buyer had. It's paying for itself. The longer it's been live, the harder it's paying.

**Duration = spend proxy.** That one reframe makes the free Meta Ad Library actionable.
