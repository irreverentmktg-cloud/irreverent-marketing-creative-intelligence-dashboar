# Steal the Signal — Competitor Gap Analysis

> What they're selling vs. what customers are actually asking for.
> Every gap below is a marketing angle.

**Last updated:** 2026-04-27
**Research sources:** Marketplace checks, Reddit r/PPC, r/FacebookAds, product review patterns

---

## The Category Map

### What Exists Today

| Product / Tool | Type | Price | What They Sell |
|---|---|---|---|
| AdSpyder | SaaS | $39–$199/mo | Ad database — search and browse competitor ads |
| PowerAdSpy | SaaS | $49–$249/mo | 7-platform spy tool — volume ad access |

| Ad research YouTube tutorials | Free | $0 | How to use tools — not how to read the output |
| Marketing agency retainers | Service | $3k–$10k/mo | Done-for-you competitive intelligence |

### What Does NOT Exist

- A **methodology** for reading the market (not just accessing it)
- A **scoring framework** that tells you which ads to actually use
- A **brief template** that converts signal into creative direction
- A **funnel audit** pairing the ad to the landing page
- A **no-subscription path** that costs $97 once and teaches the skill forever
- Content that teaches the **WHY** behind winning ads (not just the WHAT)

## Gap 1: Access vs. Literacy

### What competitors sell:
"Here are 10 million ads from your competitors."

### What customers are asking for:
"How do I know which ones are actually winning?"
"I don't know what I'm looking at."

### The gap:
Nobody teaches ad literacy. Everyone sells access to raw data. The data is useless without the reading framework.

**Our angle:** "The Meta Ad Library already shows you the data. We teach you how to read it."

---

## Gap 2: Tools vs. Thinking Process

### What competitors sell:
More data. Better filters. API connections. Automated monitoring.

### What customers are asking for:
"I have the tool but I still don't know what to test."
"I'm paying $99/month and still guessing."

### The gap:
SaaS tools automate the data collection but not the interpretation. Customers who paid $49–$299/mo still end up with a spreadsheet they don't know how to act on.

**Our angle:** "Stop buying more data. Start reading the data you already have free access to."

---

## Gap 3: Swipe Files vs. Structure

### What competitors sell:
"500 winning ads from [category]."
"The top-performing DTC ads of 2025."
Static PDFs and screenshot collections.

### What customers are asking for:
"Can you tell me WHY these ads are winning?"
"How do I adapt this to my brand?"

### The gap:
Swipe files are outputs with no process. You see the result but not the reason. The reader is left copying aesthetics instead of understanding structure.

**Our angle:** "Don't copy the creative. Steal the structure." (Structural DNA method)

---

## Gap 4: Ad-Only vs. Full Funnel

### What competitors sell:
Competitor ads. Just the ads.

### What customers are asking for:
"My CTR is good but my conversions suck."
"I don't know why the ad isn't working all the way through."

### The gap:
No competitor teaches the ad-to-landing-page audit. Everyone stops at the click. The M·05 Funnel Audit (7-point match checklist) exists nowhere else in this market.

**Our angle:** "A winning ad is only half a winning funnel. We teach the whole thing."

---

## Gap 5: One-Time Learning vs. Ongoing SaaS Dependency

### What competitors sell:
Monthly subscriptions. Recurring access. Platform lock-in.

### What customers are asking for:
"I just want to learn the method so I can do it myself."
"I don't want another tool I have to remember to check."

### The gap:
The market is full of subscription fatigue. A $97 one-time purchase that teaches a reusable skill is positioned perfectly against the "another $99/mo" objection.

**Our angle:** "$97 once. The skill is yours forever. No monthly invoice."

---

## Gap 6: Complexity vs. Repeatable Rhythm

### What competitors sell:
Feature-rich platforms with steep learning curves.
Agency retainers that require ongoing relationship management.

### What customers are asking for:
"Just show me what to do on Monday morning."
"Give me a process I can repeat without thinking."

### The gap:
No product in this space teaches a simple weekly rhythm. The M·07 "45 minutes, once a week" system exists nowhere in the competitive set.

**Our angle:** "45 minutes every Monday. That's the whole game."
