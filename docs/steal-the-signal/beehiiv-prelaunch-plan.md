# Steal the Signal — Beehiiv Pre-Launch Email Strategy

> Goal: Build 1,000 subscribers before we sell anything. Then convert at 5–10%.
> Timeline: 60–90 days to list. Then launch.

---

## The Core Idea

Don't launch to an empty room. The $97 offer needs a warm list of people who already believe in the problem. Beehiiv is the engine. The daily research is the fuel. Content from the research drives the signups.

**The flywheel:**
```
Daily research → content insight → Reels/YouTube clip → landing page signup → Beehiiv email → trust build → $97 purchase
```

---

## Phase 1: Build (Weeks 1–4) — Get to 500 Subscribers

### The Newsletter Identity

**Publication name:** Steal the Signal
**Tagline:** The weekly market intelligence brief for marketers who don't want to run blind.
**Format:** Weekly email, ~500 words, every Tuesday
**Voice:** Irreverent, direct, insider-feeling. Not a course. Not a guru. A sharp tool.

### Weekly Email Structure (every Tuesday)

```
Subject: [Signal of the week — one specific, juicy finding]

1. THE SIGNAL (150 words)
   What's resonating right now in [niche]. One specific thing.
   "This week's signal: [specific finding from daily research]"

2. WHY IT'S WORKING (100 words)
   The mechanism. The emotion. The angle.

3. HOW TO STEAL IT (150 words)
   Exact steps to apply it to their own market/niche.

4. WHAT TO WATCH (50 words)
   One thing to look for next week. Keeps them coming back.

5. CTA (50 words)
   Pre-launch: "We're building something for serious signal readers. Get early access."
   Post-launch: "The full system is $97. Get it here."
```

---

### Lead Magnet: "The Signal Starter Kit"

**Offer:** Free 5-page PDF that teaches the basics of reading market signals
**Sections:**
1. The 5 Free Intelligence Sources (Meta Library, Reddit, YouTube Search, Google Trends, TikTok Creative Center)
2. How to Read an Ad Like a Media Buyer (3-step framework)
3. The 60-Minute Pre-Spend Research Sprint
4. The 3 Questions That Reveal a Winning Angle

**Delivery:** Instant via Beehiiv automation
**Goal:** Give real value immediately so the trust bank opens on day 1

## Phase 2: Warm (Weeks 5–8) — Get to 1,000 Subscribers

### Content Strategy Escalates

- Week 5: Introduce "the system" teaser — "We're building the full Steal the Signal methodology"
- Week 6: Share a case study / before-after (real or composite) using the method
- Week 7: Run a "what's your biggest signal problem?" reply campaign — gather buyer language
- Week 8: Announce the launch date. Build urgency. Open early-bird at $77 (limited seats)

### The "Reply Campaign" (Week 7)

Subject: "Quick question before I finish this"

Body: "I'm finishing the Steal the Signal system and I want to make sure I answer the right questions. What's the ONE thing you wish you knew about reading your market before you spent money on ads? Reply and tell me. I read every response."

**What this does:**
- Gets you exact buyer language for the sales page
- Signals you're listening (trust builder)
- Creates investment — they told you what they want, now they want to see if you delivered

## The Daily Research → Content Pipeline

Every day's research log feeds the content pipeline. Here's how:

```
Daily tracker finding
        ↓
Instagram Reel (same week — post within 48 hours of finding)
        ↓
Tuesday newsletter (aggregate best findings of the week)
        ↓
YouTube Short (longer explanation, SEO value)
        ↓
Twitter thread (repurpose Tuesday email)
        ↓
Reddit post (genuine share, no selling — link in profile)
```

**The rule:** If something in the daily research makes you say "wait, people need to know this" — that's your content. Don't overthink it. Post it.
