## CAROUSEL 5 — "The Funnel Nobody Audits"
**Theme:** The M·05 reveal — ad is only half the funnel
**Best for:** LinkedIn (resonates with agency/in-house audience), Instagram
**CTA target:** Direct sale

---

**SLIDE 1 (Hook)**
```
Most brands copy the winning ad.

Then wonder why their ROAS doesn't move.

Here's what they're missing.
```

---

**SLIDE 2**
```
An ad's job: earn a click.
A page's job: earn a purchase.

A winning ad on a broken page
is a brand burning CAC without knowing why.
```

---

**SLIDE 3**
```
The 7-point funnel match audit:

1. Hook continuity — does the page open with the ad's hook?
2. Offer match — is the offer visible above the fold?
3. Visual continuity — same product/scene as the ad?
4. CTA congruence — does the button verb match?
5. Proof parity — same social proof claim?
6. Speed to commit — cart visible in one mobile scroll?
7. Price anchoring — price visible, discount reads as a saving?
```

---

**SLIDE 4**
```
7 yeses = complete funnel. Steal the whole thing.
5–6 yeses = copy their angle, out-build their page.
0–2 yeses = the ad is working despite the page.

Most competitors score 3–5.

That gap is your edge.
```

---

**SLIDE 5**
```
The most common funnel bleed:

Ad says "25% off"
Page shows 20% off

Offer drift.
Happens when promos update in the shop
but not in the ad account.

You've definitely done this.
Now you can catch it in competitors — and yourself.
```
