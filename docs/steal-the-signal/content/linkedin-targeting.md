## The LinkedIn Opportunity

LinkedIn is the only platform where the exact job titles of our buyer sit in one place. DTC operators, in-house marketing leads, and agency strategists are all heavy LinkedIn users — and they don't get pitched the competitive intelligence angle there, because every other marketer is running Facebook ads content.

LinkedIn = blue ocean for this exact offer.

## Primary Audience Segments

### Segment 1: In-House DTC Marketing Lead

**Who they are:** Marketing directors, VP Marketing, Head of Growth, CMO at DTC/e-commerce brands. They're the people inside the building who brief agencies, manage the creative calendar, and sit in uncomfortable Q4 strategy meetings.

**Job titles to target/connect with:**
- VP of Marketing (DTC / E-commerce)
- Head of Growth (DTC)
- Marketing Director (Consumer Brands)
- CMO (DTC, Consumer, E-commerce)
- Director of Performance Marketing
- Head of Paid Social
- Director of Creative Strategy

**Company types:**
- E-commerce brands ($10M–$100M revenue)
- DTC brands (beauty, wellness, fitness, home, apparel, food/bev)
- Consumer packaged goods with direct sales component

**Company size on LinkedIn:** 11–200 employees (most DTC brands in this range)

**Their LinkedIn behavior:**
- Share articles about creative performance, creative fatigue, scaling challenges
- Comment on agency/platform posts about attribution, iOS changes
- Post about team wins (campaign results) but not about process
- Connect with agency owners, media buyers, creative directors

**Pain signals to look for in their posts:**
- "We're scaling but creative is a bottleneck"
- "Our agency is great but we need better briefs"
- "Looking for recommendations on [ad intelligence tool]"
- Any post about creative testing, refresh cadence, or ad fatigue

### Segment 2: Agency Strategist / Media Buyer

**Who they are:** The account lead or strategist at a DTC-focused digital agency. Responsible for competitive analysis, creative strategy, and client reporting. Needs to justify recommendations with data.

**Job titles:**
- Paid Social Strategist
- Performance Marketing Strategist
- Senior Media Buyer (Facebook / Meta)
- Account Director (Performance / DTC)
- Creative Strategist
- Client Partner (Digital Agency)
- Head of Paid Media

**Company types:**
- Performance marketing agencies
- DTC-specialized agencies (small to mid: 10–100 employees)
- Independent media buying consultants / freelancers

**Their LinkedIn behavior:**
- Post thought leadership on ad performance, creative testing, platform changes
- Comment heavily on Meta/TikTok algorithm posts
- Share client wins (anonymized) or industry data
- Connect with brand-side marketing leads (their clients)

**Pain signals:**
- "Our pitch process needs work"
- "How do you guys structure competitive analysis for clients?"
- Posts about creative burnout, refresh cycles
- Questions about how to prove strategy recommendations to skeptical clients

**Job titles:**

**Company types:**

**Their LinkedIn behavior:**
- Follow DTC thought leaders (Andrew Foxwell, Taylor Holiday, etc.)
- Post about building in public — wins and struggles
- Ask questions in comments more than they post

## Content Strategy for LinkedIn

### Voice calibration for LinkedIn (vs. Instagram)
LinkedIn requires slightly more context than Instagram. The audience is in "work brain" mode. Lead with a specific observation or data point before the hook. Still: no fluff, no motivational poster energy, no guru clichés.

**LinkedIn tone:** Smart colleague who just found something interesting. Not a thought leader. Not a course seller.

### Content types that work on LinkedIn

| Type | Format | Post cadence |
|---|---|---|
| Short observation (3–5 lines) | Text only | 3x/week |
| Carousel (same slides as Instagram) | PDF carousel | 2x/week |
| Story post (personal, specific) | Text only, 150–300 words | 1x/week |
| Screen recording tutorial | Native video, 60–90 sec | 1x/week |

## LinkedIn Post Templates

### Template 1: Short Observation (High-frequency, 3x/week)

```
[Specific observation from research or from using the method]

[1-2 sentences expanding it]

[1 sentence implication for the reader]

---

[Optional: "More on this in Steal the Signal — link in comments"]
```

**Example:**
```
Most DTC brands audit their own ads constantly.

None of them audit the seam between their ad account and their landing page.

That gap is where CAC disappears. Not in the ad. In the mismatch.

---

Module 5 of Steal the Signal is a 7-point audit that catches this in 3 minutes.
```

---

### Template 2: The Specific Data Post

```
[Data point or specific finding — make it concrete]

Here's what this means:

→ [Implication 1]
→ [Implication 2]
→ [Implication 3]

[One-line wrap that points to the method]
```

**Example:**
```
Ad that's been running for 45 days cleared every kill threshold the media buyer had.

Here's what that means:

→ No media buyer leaves a losing ad up for 6 weeks
→ Duration is your spend proxy in the Meta Ad Library
→ You don't need impressions data — you need a start date

Sort by oldest. That's where the winners are.
```

---

### Template 3: The "What I Found" Post

```
I [action — looked at / ran / tested / reviewed] [thing].

Here's what I found:

[Numbered or bulleted findings — 3–5 max]

Most people [what they're getting wrong about this].

[Closing line that challenges them or offers the solution]
```

**Example:**
```
I looked at 50 competitor ad libraries in the DTC beauty space this week.

Here's what I found:

1. 73% of "winning" ads had zero landing page offer match
2. The highest-scoring ads had an average of 6 variants — not 1
3. Problem-aware hooks dominated the Steal tier 2:1 over social proof

Most people copy the creative. They miss the structure.

The structure is what's actually working.
```

---

### Template 4: Story/Personal Post (1x/week)

```
[Specific situation — real or illustrative]

[What you tried first — the wrong approach]

[The moment you realized something was different]

[What actually worked]

[One-line takeaway the reader can use today]
```

## LinkedIn Organic Outreach Strategy

### Who to connect with (weekly — 20–30 new connections)
- VP Marketing at DTC brands you can find via company search
- Agency strategists who post about Meta/paid social
- Founders posting about their DTC brand journey
- Media buyers / performance marketers (solo or agency)

### Connection request message template
```
Hey [Name] — saw your post about [specific topic they posted about]. 
Resonated — I'm building Steal the Signal, a competitive intelligence 
system for DTC marketers who want to stop guessing what's working in 
their category. Figured we'd probably follow each other's work. 
Connecting if it's useful.
```

### Engagement strategy (15 min/day)
- Comment substantively (2–3 sentences) on 5 posts per day from target audience
- Comment on content from DTC thought leaders (Taylor Holiday, Andrew Foxwell, Nick Shackelford, Cat Howell) — these attract your exact buyer
- Ask a question in your comment when possible — drives back-and-forth

- Industries: Retail, Consumer Goods, Marketing & Advertising, Internet
- Geography: United States, Canada, United Kingdom, Australia

**Ad format:** Single image or document ad (carousel works on LinkedIn)
**CTA:** "Get the free Signal Starter Kit" → Beehiiv landing page

**Budget note:** LinkedIn CPL is high ($40–$80+). Only run when organic has validated the message and you have email revenue to cover CAC.
