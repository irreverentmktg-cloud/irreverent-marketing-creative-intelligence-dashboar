## Weekly Gut Check Questions

Answer every Monday before planning the week's content:

1. **What got the most saves/shares last week?** → Make more of that format.
2. **What got comments expressing pain?** → Log those exact words in voc-language-bank.md.
3. **Which channel drove the most Beehiiv signups?** → Increase posting frequency there.
4. **What hook got the most first-sentence engagement?** → Use that angle in the next email subject.
5. **What did I almost post but didn't?** → That's often the content worth testing.
