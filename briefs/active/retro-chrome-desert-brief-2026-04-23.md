# Campaign Brief: Retro Chrome Desert
**Date:** 2026-04-23
**Campaign ID:** retro-chrome-desert
**Status:** Active — Ready for FORGE

---

## Campaign Concept

> "Born in the Future. Forged in the Desert."

A visually arresting editorial campaign built around a single tension: **the impossibly clean chrome machine against the brutal, sun-scorched desert**. No garages. No show floors. Just open sky, cracked earth, and a car that looks like it landed from 1959's vision of 2059.

This campaign sells **chrome car care products** through pure aspiration — the product is invisible in the creative. What the viewer sees is *the result*: a mirrored surface so perfect it reflects the whole desert back at itself.

---

## Visual Identity

| Element | Direction |
|---------|-----------|
| **Aesthetic** | Retrofuturism — Jet Age meets Mojave |
| **Era References** | 1955–1965 American concept cars, NASA Mercury program, drive-in diners |
| **Color Palette** | Chrome silver, burnt orange, deep cerulean sky, heat-shimmer white |
| **Lighting** | Golden hour (5–7pm desert sun), long shadows, flares off chrome |
| **Composition** | Wide, cinematic 2.39:1 crops; car centered in vast negative space |
| **Surface Texture** | Chrome so sharp it functions as a mirror — reflects dunes, sky, horizon |

## Tone of Voice

- **Confident, not arrogant.** This brand earned the right to be this cool.
- **Spare.** Never more than 10 words in a headline. Desert has silence; so does great copy.
- **Slightly irreverent.** We respect the car, not the gatekeepers.
- **Timeless.** Write like it could have run in 1962 *or* 2062.

---

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Car enthusiasts, 28–48M, own 1+ vehicle they care about beyond transportation
**Secondary:** Style-conscious buyers, 24–38M/F, who respond to design aesthetics
**Psychographic:** Nostalgic futurists — love vintage design but buy cutting-edge products

## FORGE Prompt Guidance

When running FORGE on this brief, use these image generation parameters:

```
Style: retrofuturism, 1960s concept car aesthetic, chrome car photography
Environment: Mojave desert, salt flats, cracked earth, vast sky
Lighting: golden hour, dramatic side lighting, lens flares, long shadows
Mood: cinematic isolation, timeless cool, quiet power
Camera: wide angle, low POV, sharp focus on chrome surfaces
Color grading: warm highlights, cool shadows, slightly desaturated sky
Negative prompts: busy backgrounds, multiple cars, people in frame, modern buildings
```
