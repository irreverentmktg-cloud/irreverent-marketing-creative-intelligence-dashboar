## Key Observations

### 1. BPN is the category incumbent on Meta

With ~580 active ads, BPN owns the Creapure® purity lane and the "just works, no BS" simplicity lane. Their creative volume is enormous. Atlas is price-matched at $0.58/serving but BPN has no acetic acid story. The differentiation is real — but Atlas has to earn the right to make that claim through creative volume first.

### 2. Transparent Labs handed Atlas a free steal

Transparent Labs dropped Creapure®. Their VOC is now full of angry loyalists who feel betrayed. These are high-intent, ingredient-educated buyers who already proved willingness to pay $1.50/serving. A direct "Still Creapure®" ad has a ready-made audience.

### 3. The gummy category is growing AND failing

SWOLY (~70 ads) and Create Wellness (~45 ads) are spending on gummy creatine. Create Wellness specifically failed independent lab testing — creatinine (the degraded byproduct) detected instead of active creatine. This is a documented, public vulnerability. Atlas powder with Creapure® certification is the credibility counter-narrative.

### 4. Ekko Vision owns TikTok, not Meta

Ekko Vision has essentially zero Meta presence but 2.3M+ TikTok views on creatine content. They've built a TikTok-first brand in this category. For Meta, this is not a direct competitor. For TikTok, they are the category creative benchmark.

### 5. Zero competitors address endurance athletes

Across all 11 competitors, not one ad speaks to runners, cyclists, CrossFit athletes, or BJJ practitioners. The acetic acid / glycogen replenishment angle is entirely unclaimed territory on Meta.

### 6. Zero competitors serve women with creatine myth-busting content

Women are the fastest-growing creatine buyer segment on TikTok in 2025. Not one competitor has built dedicated female-targeted creatine creative on Meta.
