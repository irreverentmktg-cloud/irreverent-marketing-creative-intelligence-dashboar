## Category Language Patterns

**Language buyers USE (use in copy):**

- "The first set" / "by the last set"
- "I can feel it working"
- "No bloat / no puffiness"
- "Mixes clean"
- "Actually Creapure®"
- "My coach told me to take creatine" (peer authority)
- "I was skeptical about creatine" (entry objection)
- "For my BJJ / CrossFit / cycling" (sport-specific)

**Language to AVOID:**

- "Optimize your performance"
- "Take your training to the next level"
- "Unlock your potential"
- "Transform your body"
- "Science-backed formula"
