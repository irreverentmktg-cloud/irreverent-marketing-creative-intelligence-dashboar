## Control Ad Deconstruct

**Current highest-impression Atlas ad (Library ID 836773929169488, first seen Dec 11, 2025):**

> **Hook:** "Ready to unleash your true potential? 💪 This isn't 'just creatine.' It's designed for goal-crushers like you who demand more: heavier lifts, harder sessions, fuller pumps, longer stamina."
> 

> **CTA:** Learn More | **Offer:** BOGO50 | **Visual:** Product-led, brand voice
> 

### What Works

- ✅ Opens with differentiation claim ("This isn't just creatine")
- ✅ Lists multiple outcome benefits in sequence (strength, stamina, pumps)
- ✅ Has a specific offer (BOGO50)
- ✅ Running since Dec 2025 — evergreen proof

### What Fails

- ❌ "Unleash your true potential" = most generic phrase in supplement advertising
- ❌ "Goal-crushers" = 2019 gym-bro language, dated and generic
- ❌ Zero mention of Pürest Creatine® (actual quality differentiator)
- ❌ Zero mention of acetic acid (actual unique differentiator)
- ❌ No endurance angle despite this being Atlas's owned territory
- ❌ No social proof — every claim is brand voice
- ❌ Speaks to "everyone" — converts no one ICP cleanly
- ❌ No female angle, no category attack on gummies or generic powder

**Control Score: 4/10** — Differentiates but doesn't prove. Lists benefits without naming the mechanisms that earn the right to claim them.

Addresses bloat ("that's from cheap creatine with fillers"). Closes: "Six weeks on Atlas. Strength is up. Zero bloat. My hair is fine."

**CTA:** *"30-day money back. Start whenever you're ready →"*

**Notes:** Must-produce. Zero Ambrosia Atlas creative addresses this buyer. Fastest-growing TikTok segment. First-mover window.

## 5-Stage Awareness Map

### Stage 1 — Unaware

**Hook:** *"If you're doing CrossFit and not taking creatine, you're leaving performance on the table — but not for the reason you think."*

**Visual:** Athlete mid-WOD, gassed, text overlay: “Most creatine is for powerlifters. That's the problem.”

**Format:** 60s educational reel. Lead with the category reframe. Atlas product reveal at 0:30.

**CTA:** *"Learn what creatine actually does for endurance →"*

### Stage 2 — Problem-Aware

**Hook:** *"I was scared to try creatine for 3 years. Here's what I wish someone told me sooner."*

**Format:** UGC, Ashley archetype. Myth debunk first, outcome close. Female creator mandatory.

**CTA:** *"Start with one tub. 30-day money back →"*

### Stage 3 — Solution-Aware

**Primary hook:** *"I've been using basic creatine for 4 years. Here's why I switched."*

**Alt hook (ingredient buyer):** *"No compromises on creatine sourcing. Pürest Creatine® + acetic acid."*

**Format:** Comparison video or direct steal static. Derek archetype.

**CTA:** *"Same price. Different formula →"*

### Stage 4 — Product-Aware

**Line A hook:** *"'The tub looked half empty' — here's why (and what you actually get)."*

**Line B hook:** *"46% of creatine gummies failed lab testing. Here's why powder wins."*

**Format:** Two educational pieces. Factual, calm. Trust-building.

**CTA:** *"Try risk-free — 30-day money back →"*

### Stage 5 — Most-Aware

**Format:** Retargeting static. Walter P. stack reveal OR bundle offer (3-Pack at 30-day guarantee).

**CTA:** *"Your 30-day trial starts whenever you're ready."*
