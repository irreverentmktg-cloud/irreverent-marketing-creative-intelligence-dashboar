## 🎬 Video Script

**Hook (0–3 sec):** Your sugar cravings have nothing to do with willpower. They're your body asking for something specific — and it's not what you think.

**Setup (3–10 sec):** Most people fight cravings with discipline. The smarter move is to understand what the craving is actually telling you.

**Point 1 (10–20 sec):** When blood sugar drops, your body needs fast energy. But the real culprit is usually low protein at your last meal. Protein slows glucose absorption and keeps blood sugar stable for hours — skip it, and your body starts signaling for sugar to compensate.

**Point 2 (20–30 sec):** Protein also drives satiety hormones like peptide YY and GLP-1 — the same hormones that weight-loss drugs like Ozempic mimic. Eat enough protein and those cravings don't show up in the first place.

**Point 3 (30–45 sec):** The fix is front-loading protein in your first meal of the day. Aim for 30–40g at breakfast — eggs, Greek yogurt, or a protein smoothie. People who hit this target report dramatically fewer afternoon cravings within one week.

**Bridge (45–55 sec):** Next time you want something sweet, eat 20g of protein first and wait 20 minutes. The craving either disappears or drops by half. Try it once and you'll understand what your body was asking for.

**CTA (55–60 sec):** Save this for the next time it hits. Follow for more nutrition facts that replace willpower with understanding.
