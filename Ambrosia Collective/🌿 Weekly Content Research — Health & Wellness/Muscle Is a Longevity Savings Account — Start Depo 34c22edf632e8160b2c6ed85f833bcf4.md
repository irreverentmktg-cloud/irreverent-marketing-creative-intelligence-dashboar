**Point 1 (10–20 sec):** After 30, you lose 3–5% of your muscle mass every decade. After 60, that rate accelerates. Research now supports consuming roughly 1 gram per pound of body weight, especially as you age. You can't build what you don't feed, and most people are dramatically under-eating protein relative to what their muscle maintenance actually requires.
