## 🎬 Video Script

**Hook (0–3 sec):** If you're exhausted, foggy, and your sleep doesn't help — your diet probably has a gap you don't know about yet.

**Setup (3–10 sec):** Fatigue is the most common complaint doctors hear. And most of the time, nobody checks for the five nutrients that cause it.

**Point 1 (10–20 sec):** Magnesium deficiency affects over 300 enzymatic pathways in your body — including the ones that control energy production and sleep quality. Low magnesium means your cells literally can't make energy efficiently.

**Point 2 (20–30 sec):** Vitamin D isn't just a bone vitamin. It regulates mood, immune response, and testosterone production. Most people in northern climates are deficient without knowing it — and blood tests almost never catch it early.

**Point 3 (30–45 sec):** B12 is the one that surprises people. It's only found in animal products, so vegetarians and vegans deplete it slowly over years. By the time you notice brain fog and numbness, your stores have been low for months.

**Bridge (45–55 sec):** Get a full micronutrient panel — not just a standard blood test. Ask specifically for magnesium RBC, vitamin D, B12, ferritin, and zinc. Most GPs won't order these unless you ask.

**CTA (55–60 sec):** Save this before your next doctor's appointment. Follow for more functional nutrition facts.
