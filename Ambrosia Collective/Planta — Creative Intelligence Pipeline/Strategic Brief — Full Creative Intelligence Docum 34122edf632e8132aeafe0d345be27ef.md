### The #1 Category Gap

Not one competitor is running a **male UGC creator for plant protein on Meta**. Truvani has 410 ads — all female. Clean Simple Eats has 130 — all female. Gains In Bulk has 180 — all creatine. KOS has 6 — no creators. Momentous has Arnold — but he's a celebrity, not a peer.

A single authentic male talking-head ad is the first of its kind in this category.

### The Urgent Competitive Alert

Momentous launched Arnold Schwarzenegger into Planta's exact audience on **March 31, 2026**. Their angles — plant protein, gut health, no gimmicks, performance stack — are a direct ICP overlap. The counter must be more human and more personal than a celebrity endorsement.

### What Makes Planta Defensible

- **SunBCAA™** — sunflower-derived BCAAs at 2:1:1 leucine ratio. No competitor is running this story.
- **Digestive enzyme blend** — makes the gut health claim functional, not just a claim.
- **27 vitamins & minerals** — legitimate multivitamin replacement. "This replaced two things in my stack" is a unique creative credential.
- **Flavor names** — Banana Maple French Toast, Cinnamon Roll, Peanut Butter Cup. The most ambitious flavor naming in the plant protein category.
