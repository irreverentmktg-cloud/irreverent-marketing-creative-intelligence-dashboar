### Top 3 Emotional Drivers

**Driver 1 — "I finally found the one that tastes good" (Taste Relief)**

Every plant protein buyer has a graveyard of bad experiences. Gritty textures, grassy aftertastes, fake sweetness. When Planta works for them, the emotion isn't satisfaction — it's *relief*. "I didn't think plant protein could taste like this" is the conversion moment. Flavor is the product truth that unlocks every other claim.

**Driver 2 — "My body actually feels right after this" (Digestion + Comfort)**

Whey bloat is a known, accepted suffering in the fitness community — until someone experiences the alternative. Buyers who switch to Planta often describe the absence of heaviness, bloat, and discomfort as a revelation. They weren't looking for gut health; they stumbled into it.

**Driver 3 — "I'm not compromising on performance" (Efficacy Validation)**

The male plant protein buyer has a chip on his shoulder. He switched from whey and he needs to know he didn't trade results for comfort. SunBCAA™ and the amino profile are the proof he's looking for. "Gets in my macros without making me feel like crap" is the entire purchase logic in one line.

### Top 3 Objections

**Objection 1 — "Plant protein doesn't actually build muscle"**

The deepest male objection. Years of gym culture have embedded "whey = gains" as fact. The counter is SunBCAA™ leucine ratio — science that removes the objection without asking him to take it on faith.

**Objection 2 — "It's probably going to taste terrible"**

The #1 category-wide objection. Every man in the ICP has tried at least one plant protein that tasted like wet grass or chalk. The objection isn't irrational — it's earned. Taste proof must come in the first 3 seconds.

**Objection 3 — "I already have protein — do I really need to switch?"**

Inertia. He's not in pain enough to change. The gut health hook is the pattern interrupt — naming a discomfort he's accepted as normal and giving him permission to expect more.

## STEP 3 — ICP AVATAR

### ICP 1 — Marcus (Primary · 32–42)

**Lifestyle:** Trains 3–5x/week. Protein shake is non-negotiable. Whey is his default — he's used it for years. He's heard plant protein is better now but every time he's tried it, it was chalky, grassy, or both. He threw a bag in the trash after the second scoop. He's health-curious (Huberman listener, maybe tried AG1), not a hardcore bodybuilder but takes his macros seriously.

**Trigger:** Notices the post-workout heaviness he's been ignoring for years. Or his girlfriend mentions bloating. Or a Reddit thread connects whey to gut issues. Or he sees one guy on his feed genuinely raving about a plant protein — not an ad, a real guy.

**Silent Fear:** If he switches to plant protein, he'll lose gains. He's built what he has on whey. Switching feels like a risk he's not sure about.

**Current Stack:** Whey protein (Gold Standard or similar), creatine, pre-workout, maybe fish oil. $80–130/month. No multivitamin — he thinks he doesn't need one.

**What He's Tried + Failed:** One bag of Vega (threw it out). Maybe Orgain (texture). Considered Momentous but the price killed it.

**The Problem He Hasn't Named:** The bloat, heaviness, and post-shake discomfort are accepted as "just how it is." Nobody has told him that feeling isn't mandatory. When he hears SunBCAA™ has the same leucine ratio as whey — and sees a real guy who looks like him saying "no bloat, same gains" — the switch clicks.

**How to win him:** Taste proof first (3 seconds). Performance proof second (SunBCAA™ story). Remove the switch risk (30-day MBG).

**Creative instinct:** He doesn't respond to celebrity. He responds to a peer. One real guy, iPhone, kitchen, honest first reaction. That's the entire ad.
