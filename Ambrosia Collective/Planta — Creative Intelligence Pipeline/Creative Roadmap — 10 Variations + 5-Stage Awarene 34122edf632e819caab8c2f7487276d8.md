## 5-Stage Awareness Map — Summary

| Stage | Who They Are | Core Message | Hook Direction |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 — Unaware | Lives with bloat, hasn't named it | "That heavy feeling isn't normal" | Interrupt → name the pain |
| 2 — Problem-Aware | Knows whey is the issue, exploring options | "Plant protein is better now — but most brands haven't caught up" | Validate → bridge to solution |
| 3 — Solution-Aware | Knows plant protein exists, skeptical of taste/efficacy | "This one solved both problems" | Overcome objections |
| 4 — Product-Aware | Has seen Planta, hasn't bought yet | "Here's exactly why it's different" | SunBCAA + enzyme story + social proof |
| 5 — Most-Aware | Repeat buyer, or close to purchasing | "Subscribe & Save, don't run out" | Loyalty + value |
