## What We Found

### Truvani (~410 active ads)

- **Oldest ad:** Dec 12, 2025
- **Creative velocity:** ~25–35 new ads/month
- **All creators:** Female (Dee Bailey, Melanie Duncan, Holistically Stevi, Food Babe)
- **Dominant mechanic:** 72% OFF Starter Kit as the lead hook on majority of ads
- **Secondary angles:** 11 flavor variety showcase, "No Gums. No Stevia." clean label, price objection handling ("worried about price? I got good news")
- **CTA:** Shop Now universally
- **Audience:** 100% female-coded. Zero male content.
- **Read:** Truvani is betting their entire growth engine on Meta discounting + female UGC. The male plant protein buyer is completely invisible to them.

### Gains In Bulk (~180 active ads)

- **Important:** Nearly 100% creatine, not protein. Remove from protein competitor analysis.
- **Creators:** Clark Bartram (male)
- **Angles:** Bloat-free creatine, 97% water soluble, tone + muscle
- **Read:** Not a Meta plant protein competitor. Cross off.

### Clean Simple Eats (~130 active ads)

- **Oldest ad:** Jul 28, 2025
- **Creative velocity:** ~10–15/month, spiking in April 2026
- **All creators:** Female (Stay Fit Mom, shandi.lynn.martin)
- **Dominant mechanic:** New flavor launches (Campfire Mallow Sweetness) + Target retail launch April 10, 2026
- **Angles:** "Build lean muscle, curb cravings," macro-balanced lifestyle, recipe integration
- **CTA:** Shop Now
- **Read:** Best flavor + lifestyle creative playbook in the category — but all female, all whey. Nobody has run this for men + plant protein.

### KOS Nutritionals (~6 active ads)

- **Oldest ad:** Jan 7, 2026
- **Creative velocity:** ~1–2/month
- **No influencer creators visible — brand-only**
- **Ads running:** (1) "We heard you. We're back." Amazon restock + 25% Subscribe & Save, (2) FREE 3-pack sample trial, (3) "Plant Protein, Without the Bloat" video, (4) "$0.61 per cup" BOGO on Sippin' On Shroomboom mushroom coffee
- **Read:** $41M revenue brand running 6 ads. Meta is essentially dark for them. Massive opportunity to capture their wellness audience — they're not defending it.

### Momentous (~20 active ads) ⚠️

- **Launch date:** March 31, 2026 — coordinated burst
- **Oldest ad:** March 5, 2026
- **Creative velocity:** ~15–20/month (burst pattern)
- **Lead creator:** Arnold Schwarzenegger (with and without Momentous branding)
- **Also:** Dr. Stacy Sims, The Protein Powder Guru, Momentous brand
- **Angles:**
    - "The Momentous Three" — Protein + Creatine + Omega-3 bundle, now 100% plant-based
    - "It's always about the basics. Protein. Creatine. Vitamin D3. No gimmicks."
    - "Clinically backed and synergistically formulated to support energy, strength, and cognitive clarity"
    - "Plant Protein, Without the Bloat"
    - Fiber+ / gut health as performance foundation: "Your gut supports your results"
    - "The true foundation for your performance might not be what you think"
- **CTA:** Shop Now, Learn More
- **Read:** Direct ICP overlap with Planta. Arnold + gut health + plant protein + no-gimmicks is exactly Marcus. They have $45M+. The counter needs to be more human, more personal, less celebrity.

## The Single Biggest Gap

> Across 410 + 180 + 130 + 6 + 20 ads — **not one competitor is running a male UGC creator for plant protein on Meta.** The first brand to do it owns the format.
