## 🔑 Key Finding: TikTok Ad Library ≠ Meta Ad Library

TikTok's Ad Library ([library.tiktok.com](http://library.tiktok.com)) only indexes political/issue ads and select large advertisers — **it does NOT show commercial DTC ads the way Meta's library does.** All protein supplement brands run through **Spark Ads** (boosted organic creator content) and **creator affiliate programs** — none surface in the formal library.

This means:

- **No competitor has a transparent paid ad infrastructure advantage on TikTok.** The moat is creator relationships, not ad creative.
- The intelligence signal isn't "who's running what ad" — it's **"what angles are creators proving out organically that will move to paid in 3–6 months."**
- Planta has the same access to TikTok's growth levers as every competitor. The window is open.

## Forward Signals Summary

### Angles Running on TikTok → Not Yet on Meta

**1. Anti-Bloat is the #1 hook in plant protein on TikTok right now**

The "bloat bowl" trend went viral in 2025. Content positioning plant protein as specifically anti-bloat, gut-friendly, and digestion-safe is getting massive organic traction. No competitor is running this as a *paid* angle on Meta yet. Planta's digestive enzymes + SunBCAA™ absorption is a direct fit.

**2. "I cancelled 3 supplements" / Stack Simplification**

TikTok health creators are driving heavy engagement with content about *simplifying their supplement stack.* The angle is "this one product replaced X, Y, Z." Planta's 27 vitamins + minerals is tailor-made for this — nobody is running it as a paid creative.

**3. Skeptic-to-convert male UGC**

"I swore I'd never switch from whey" storytelling format is unowned in the plant protein category on TikTok. Momentous has the closest version (male, performance), but it's about general supplements, not plant protein specifically. The male plant protein conversion story gap exists on TikTok too.

**4. Digestive enzyme differentiator**

Creators calling out *specific ingredients* that prevent bloat (enzymes, no artificial sweeteners, no emulsifiers) are driving outsized engagement. "So Lean & So Clean" and similar brands are getting format traction by explaining *why* their protein doesn't bloat you. Planta's enzyme blend = untapped creative angle.
