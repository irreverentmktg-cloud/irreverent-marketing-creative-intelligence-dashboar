### Objection Language (what to pre-empt)

**Objections to address in creative:**

1. **"Plant protein tastes bad"** → Lead with taste proof. Verbatim: "best tasting vegan protein I've ever had"
2. **"Plant protein is gritty and chalky"** → Digestive enzyme story + mixability claims + smooth-as-whey language
3. **"It's too expensive"** → Anchor against $2/serving with the quality gap. Position as the upgrade worth making.
4. **"Does plant protein even work?"** → SunBCAA™ + amino profile story + label accuracy guarantee
5. **"The sweetener feels artificial"** → Some users flagged gums + synthetic sweeteners — be honest or use flavor-specific ads that overcome with taste proof
6. **"I already tried [Orgain/Vega/Garden of Life] and hated it"** → This is the most powerful angle. See competitor map below.

**Flavor-level nuances to know:**

- Melted Chocolate = highest quality flavor (A grade)
- Cinnamon Roll = well-received, "like liquid French toast"
- Peanut Butter = weaker flavor execution — some noted "burnt peanut" notes
- Banana Maple French Toast = top-seller variant on Tiger Fitness
- S'mores = earthiness of proteins came through — not recommended for pure taste ads

## Competitor VOC — Weakness Intelligence

**What their customers hate:**

- *"Worst-tasting protein powder I've ever had"*

- *"Never fully dissolves — clumps everywhere"*
- *"Causes heartburn, gas, and bloating"*
- *"Erythritol GI distress is real"*
- *"Disgusting aftertaste from the erythritol"*

**Planta hook angle:** "The protein that doesn't bloat you, clump, or taste like regret"

### 🔴 Vega (Primary Target)

**What their customers hate:**
