## Control Ad Deconstruct

**Current highest-impression Nektar ad (Dec 23, 2025):**

> **Headline:** "Upgrade Your Body's Real Support System"
> 

> **Copy:** "Tired of greens powders that don't deliver? Upgrade to Nektar, the all-in-one superfood formula that actually works! • 13 calibrated superfoods for heart, liver & kidney support • Clean formula, no junk — just one scoop a day. Feel the difference for yourself!"
> 

> **CTA:** Shop Now | **Visual:** Outdoor/nature lifestyle, product tub in grass
> 

### What Works

- ✅ Names the organs (heart, liver, kidney)
- ✅ Mentions 13 superfoods
- ✅ Acknowledges taste ("a flavor people love")
- ✅ Problem-lead hook ("tired of greens powders that don't deliver")

### What Fails

- ❌ "That actually works" and "feel the difference" = category-generic
- ❌ No category contrast — doesn't explain *why* Nektar is different
- ❌ Taste buried in sub, never proved on camera
- ❌ No mechanism education — why do these 13 superfoods matter?
- ❌ Nature/grass visual is ironic for a brand attacking "tastes like grass"
- ❌ No social proof, no review count, no guarantee mention

**Control Score: 5/10** — Strong bones, no differentiation engine.

### VAR 4 — Medication Safety FAQ 🔴 P0

**Stage:** Product-Aware (4) | **ICP:** Marcus (45–60 subset on prescription meds) | **Format:** Talking-head video or FAQ carousel

**Variable:** Audience + hook (medication safety)

**Hook:** *"Can you take Nektar if you're on blood pressure medication?"*

**Script brief:**

Factual, calm delivery. Covers: Nektar uses whole-food superfoods, not pharmaceutical compounds. CoQ10 actually supports healthy blood pressure. Red Yeast Rice is the one to flag for statin users (Monacolin K — check with doctor). For ACE inhibitors, beta-blockers, diuretics: no known interaction. Loop in your doctor if you're on any prescription medication, but this is the answer you've been looking for.

**CTA:** *"Questions? We answer everything at [FAQ link]"*

**Targeting:** Facebook/Instagram, 45–65, interests: heart health, blood pressure, cholesterol management
