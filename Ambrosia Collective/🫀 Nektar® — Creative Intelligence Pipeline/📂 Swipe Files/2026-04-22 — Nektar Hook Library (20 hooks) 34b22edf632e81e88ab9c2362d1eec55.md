## 1. Problem Agitation

### 1.1 — Family-history trigger

**Hook:** “Dad had the heart attack at 58. You’re 41 and pretending that doesn’t mean anything.”  `[TEST FIRST]`

- **Trigger:** Identity / Shock
- **Awareness stage:** Problem-Aware
- **Visual:** Static photo of an older man’s hospital wristband on a bedside table, slow zoom. Cut to product on a kitchen counter next to a coffee mug. No actor on camera — let the viewer fill in their own face.

### 1.2 — Supplement drawer

**Hook:** “Six bottles in the supplement drawer. Still no idea if any of them are doing the one thing that actually keeps you alive.”

- **Trigger:** Negative Constraint
- **Awareness stage:** Solution-Aware
- **Visual:** Top-down shot of a junk-drawer cabinet — 6+ supplement bottles dumped out, sweep them aside, single tub of Nektar lands.

### 1.3 — The liver bill

**Hook:** “Your liver finished processing the weekend before you finished your Sunday coffee. It’s been carrying you for 40 years and you’ve never sent it anything back.”

- **Trigger:** Shock
- **Awareness stage:** Problem-Aware
- **Visual:** UGC-style man pouring coffee at 7am, voiceover reads the line. Bottle of Milk Thistle next to scoop of Nektar — “this one ingredient is what your liver wishes you’d been taking.”

### 1.4 — “Keep an eye on that”

**Hook:** “Your last physical came back with a ‘let’s keep an eye on that.’ That’s not a clean bill — that’s a deadline.”

- **Trigger:** Shock / Identity
- **Awareness stage:** Problem-Aware
- **Visual:** Tight crop on a medical chart with red highlighter circling a number. Hand sweeps the chart aside, scoop of Nektar drops into a glass of water.

## 5. Direct Call-Out

**Hook:** “Guys over 40 — if your dad had heart trouble, this is the supplement you should’ve started taking three years ago.”

- **Trigger:** Identity / FOMO
- **Awareness stage:** Problem-Aware
