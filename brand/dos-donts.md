# Creative Rules — Dos & Don'ts

## Copy Rules

### DO
- Lead with a specific hook in the first 3 seconds (video) or headline (static)
- Use real numbers: percentages, dollar amounts, time frames
- Make one clear claim per ad — don't stack multiple offers
- Match the energy of the persona (see personas.md)
- Use "you" and "your" — speak directly to the viewer
- End with a single, clear CTA
- Keep body copy under 125 characters for Meta feed placements

### DON'T
- Make guaranteed income or results claims ("make $10K/month guaranteed")
- Use before/after medical or weight loss imagery that violates Meta policies
- Stack multiple offers or CTAs in one ad
- Use ALL CAPS for more than 3 words
- Reference competitor brand names without legal clearance
- Make claims that require substantiation you don't have
- Use clickbait that doesn't deliver on its promise

## Visual Rules

### DO
- Use high-contrast visuals with a clear focal point
- Show the product or result in use — lifestyle over product-on-white
- Keep text overlay minimal (under 20% of image area for Meta)
- Use authentic-looking imagery — raw UGC style outperforms polished stock
- Test bright, attention-grabbing color palettes for feed placements

### DON'T
- Use heavily filtered or obviously AI-generated faces for testimonials
- Place critical text near edges (gets cut off on mobile)
- Use red/green color combinations (accessibility issue, also flags spam filters)
- Overlay text on busy backgrounds — contrast matters
- Use stock photos that look obviously staged

## Meta Policy Compliance

Always avoid:
- Personal attributes targeting (implying knowledge of race, religion, health, financial status)
- Shocking or sensational imagery
- Adult content
- Misleading claims about products or services
- Countdown timers that reset (fake urgency)

## Video-Specific Rules

### DO
- Hook in first 1–3 seconds — no slow intros
- Design for sound-off: captions or on-screen text that tells the story silently
- Vertical format (9:16) for Stories/Reels
- Show transformation or result by the 10-second mark
- End card with clear CTA and branding

### DON'T
- Open with a logo or brand name (wastes the hook)
- Use jump cuts faster than 1 per second (disorienting)
- Rely on music to carry emotional weight — most viewers have sound off
- Exceed 30 seconds for prospecting ads (15s is ideal)
