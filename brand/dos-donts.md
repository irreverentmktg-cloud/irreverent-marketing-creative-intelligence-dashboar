# Creative Rules — Dos & Don'ts

> The brand is the filter. Everything goes through it.

---

## Visual Dos

- **Do** shoot chrome as a mirror — reflections of landscapes, skies, horizons
- **Do** use wide, cinematic compositions with the car as a solitary subject
- **Do** lean into golden hour / blue hour desert light — drama is the point
- **Do** allow negative space to breathe — emptiness communicates confidence
- **Do** use extreme close-ups of chrome to show texture and perfection
- **Do** let the environment (desert, sky, cracked earth) be a character
- **Do** apply film grain or slight analog texture — we are not a sterile brand

## Visual Don'ts

- **Don't** put more than one car in frame unless it's a deliberate compositional choice
- **Don't** use white seamless or studio backgrounds — we live in the real world
- **Don't** show busy environments (cities, parking lots, dealerships)
- **Don't** use stock-feeling lighting — no flat overhead fluorescent energy
- **Don't** put people in the hero shot unless they are a specific persona feature
- **Don't** over-saturate — let the chrome do the work
- **Don't** use obvious CGI chrome if photorealism is available

---

## Copy Dos

- **Do** write for a second read — the first line hooks, the second pays off
- **Do** use concrete nouns over abstract ones ("cracked earth" not "harsh conditions")
- **Do** write in the active voice, present or past tense — no future-tense promises
- **Do** trust the visual to carry context — copy doesn't have to explain the image
- **Do** let silence be part of the copy strategy — sometimes no text is the right move

## Copy Don'ts

- **Don't** use category clichés: "shine," "gleam," "sparkle," "dazzle"
- **Don't** make comparative claims without backup data
- **Don't** write body copy over 40 words for feed ads
- **Don't** use emojis in editorial campaigns
- **Don't** mention competitor brands — ever
- **Don't** make claims that require legal asterisks in the creative (move those to landing pages)

---

## Brand Boundary Cases

| Scenario | Decision |
|----------|----------|
| Humor in copy | **Allowed** if dry and earned — not slapstick |
| Featuring a specific car model | **Allowed** — retro American + modern performance only |
| Desert location only | **Preferred** for this campaign; other environments on different campaigns |
| Showing the product on screen | **Optional** — aspirational-only creatives are approved |
| User-generated content | **Allowed** if it meets visual bar — review against this doc |
| Before/after content | **Allowed** and encouraged — powerful hook format |

---

## Quality Bar

Before any creative exits `ads/pending/`, ask:

1. Would this creative stop the scroll on its own visual merit?
2. Does the copy (if any) sound like it belongs in a magazine, not a flyer?
3. Does it reflect (literally or figuratively) the quality of the product?
4. Would we be proud to show this to someone who doesn't know what we sell?

If the answer to any of these is no — back to FORGE.
