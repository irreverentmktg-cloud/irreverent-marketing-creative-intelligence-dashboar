# Irreverent Marketing — Creative Dos & Don'ts

## Creative Dos

### Hooks
- [ ] Open with a pattern interrupt (unexpected stat, bold claim, or provocative question)
- [ ] Address the viewer directly ("You're wasting money on ads if...")
- [ ] Lead with the outcome, not the product ("I went from 0 to $40k/mo with one creative tweak")
- [ ] Use native-feeling formats (talking head, B-roll + VO, testimonial, screen record)

### Copy
- [ ] Keep primary text under 125 characters for feed placements
- [ ] Use numbers and specificity ("47% higher CTR" not "way better results")
- [ ] Match the energy of the platform (casual for Reels, slightly more polished for Feed)
- [ ] CTA must be singular — one action per ad

### Visuals
- [ ] First 3 seconds must work on mute
- [ ] Subtitles on all video content
- [ ] Faces perform better than product-only — use when possible
- [ ] Test high-contrast text overlays for thumb-stopping power

---

## Creative Don'ts

### Hooks
- [ ] Don't open with the brand name or logo
- [ ] Don't start with "We are..." or "Introducing..."
- [ ] Don't use clickbait that doesn't deliver on the landing page promise
- [ ] Don't run the same hook for more than 14 days without testing a variant

### Copy
- [ ] No jargon that a non-marketer wouldn't immediately understand
- [ ] No passive voice in CTAs ("Click here to learn more" → "Get the breakdown")
- [ ] No walls of text — if it needs a paragraph, it needs a new format
- [ ] Never make a claim you can't back up with data or a testimonial

### Visuals
- [ ] No stock photo energy — real > polished
- [ ] No unbranded creatives going live (subtle brand mark minimum)
- [ ] No creative running past a 3+ frequency without refreshing
- [ ] Never use a killed creative format again without understanding why it died

---

## Kill Thresholds

A creative gets killed when:
- CTR < 0.8% after 72 hours and $50+ spend
- ROAS < 1.5x after 7 days and $200+ spend
- Frequency > 3.5 with declining CTR
- Creative flagged by Meta policy review

---

## Winner Criteria

A creative is flagged as a winner when:
- ROAS ≥ 3.5x over 7+ days
- CTR ≥ 2% sustained over 72 hours
- CPC ≤ $1.20 at scale (>$100 spend)
- Hooks from winning creatives get documented in `/performance/winners/`
