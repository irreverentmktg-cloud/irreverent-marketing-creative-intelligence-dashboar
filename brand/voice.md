# Brand Voice — Irreverent Marketing

## Core Personality

Bold. Direct. A little provocative. We say what others won't.

We're not corporate. We're not polished for the sake of it. We speak like a sharp friend who happens to know exactly what they're talking about — and isn't afraid to call things out.

## Voice Attributes

| Attribute | We Are | We Are Not |
|-----------|--------|------------|
| Tone | Confident, punchy, real | Aggressive, rude, or arrogant |
| Energy | High-energy, urgent | Frantic or desperate |
| Clarity | Direct, plain-spoken | Jargon-heavy or corporate |
| Humor | Dry, knowing, occasionally irreverent | Cheesy, forced, or offensive |
| Authority | Expert who's been in the trenches | Ivory-tower theorist |

## Writing Principles

1. **Lead with the hook.** The first line must earn the second. No warmup, no preamble.
2. **Short sentences win.** Break up ideas. Create rhythm. Make it scannable.
3. **Specificity beats vagueness.** "3.5x ROAS in 11 days" > "great results"
4. **Active voice always.** "We built this" not "This was built by us."
5. **Cut the fluff.** If a word doesn't earn its place, kill it.
6. **Speak to one person.** Write for the specific persona, not "everyone."

## Example Copy — On-Brand vs Off-Brand

**Off:** "Our innovative solution leverages cutting-edge AI to optimize your marketing ROI across multiple channels."

**On:** "Stop guessing what works. This scores your ads and tells you exactly which ones to kill."

---

**Off:** "We're excited to share our new product with you today!"

**On:** "Most ad accounts are bleeding money on creatives that stopped working 3 weeks ago. Here's how to fix it."

## Phrases We Use

- "Here's what's actually happening..."
- "Stop [bad thing]. Start [good thing]."
- "Most [people/brands] get this wrong."
- "The data doesn't lie."
- Numbers, percentages, time frames — always specific

## Phrases We Avoid

- "Unlock your potential"
- "Game-changing" / "revolutionary" / "disruptive"
- "Synergy" / "leverage" (as a verb) / "paradigm"
- "We're passionate about..."
- "At the end of the day..."
