# Irreverent Marketing — Brand Voice

## Core Voice Pillars

| Pillar | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| **Irreverent** | We challenge conventions. We say what others won't. We'd rather be polarizing than forgettable. |
| **Direct** | We don't hedge. Short sentences. Active voice. No buzzword soup. |
| **Confident** | We know our stuff. No "maybe" or "might" — we make claims and back them up. |
| **Human** | We write like a smart friend, not a corporation. Contractions. Casual. Real. |

---

## Tone Spectrum

```
PLAYFUL ←————————————→ SERIOUS
         ↑
    We live here
    (leaning playful but grounded in data)
```

---

## Voice in Practice

**Do write like this:**
- "This ad died in 48 hours. Here's why."
- "Stop guessing. Start running winners."
- "Your creative isn't the problem. Your brief is."

**Don't write like this:**
- "Our innovative solution leverages cutting-edge AI to optimize your marketing synergies."
- "We're excited to announce a paradigm-shifting approach to creative excellence."
- "Results may vary. Past performance does not guarantee future results."

---

## Vocabulary

**Use:** hook, brief, winner, kill, ship, iterate, test, data, proof, signal, conversion
**Avoid:** synergy, leverage (as a verb), solution, journey, ecosystem, empower, storytelling (overused)

---

## Writing Rules

1. Lead with the insight, not the setup
2. Max 2 sentences before the point lands
3. Numbers beat adjectives ("3.8x ROAS" beats "incredible results")
4. If it sounds like an agency wrote it, rewrite it
5. Read it out loud — if you wouldn't say it, don't write it
