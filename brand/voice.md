# Brand Voice — Irreverent Marketing

> We don't make ads. We make things people screenshot.

---

## Core Voice Attributes

| Attribute | What It Means | What It's Not |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| **Confident** | We state, we don't hedge | Arrogant, dismissive |
| **Spare** | Every word earns its place | Minimalist to the point of cold |
| **Irreverent** | We question the rules of advertising | Reckless or offensive |
| **Cinematic** | We think in images, not features | Vague or abstract |
| **Timeless** | Copy that could run in any decade | Trendy slang, dated references |

---

## Voice by Campaign Type

### Editorial / Aspirational
Write like a great magazine photographer speaks — in visuals first, words second. The image does the heavy lifting. Copy exists to land the feeling, not explain the product.

**Tone:** Quiet authority. Long pauses. One great line.
**Example:** *"The desert remembers everything. Your chrome should too."*

### Problem / Solution
Direct, honest, slightly conspiratorial. We're on the customer's side, not the brand's.

**Tone:** Like advice from someone who actually knows their stuff.
**Example:** *"Out here, the desert takes everything. We built this so it doesn't take your shine."*

### Pattern Interrupt / Scroll-Stopper
Provocative but not cheap. The goal is a double-take, not offense.

**Tone:** Unexpected. One beat of confusion, then the payoff.
**Example:** *"Chrome doesn't rust. Neither does obsession."*

## Copy Rules

1. **Headlines: 10 words max.** If it needs more, cut it down.
2. **No adjective stacking.** One strong noun beats three weak modifiers.
3. **Avoid "premium," "luxury," "best," "ultimate."** Show it, don't claim it.
4. **No exclamation marks in body copy.** Confidence doesn't shout.
5. **Read it aloud.** If it sounds like an ad, rewrite it.
6. **Timeless over trending.** No slang with a shelf life.

---

## Phrases We Love

- Compression. One idea. Total.
- A line that earns a second read.
- Visual-first: the copy lands what the image set up.
- Copy that would look good on a poster, not just a feed.

## Phrases We Avoid

- "Game-changing" / "revolutionary" / "next-level"
- Rhetorical questions that aren't actually surprising
- Emojis in editorial contexts
- Hashtags in ad copy (belongs in post metadata, not the creative)
- "Join the X community"
