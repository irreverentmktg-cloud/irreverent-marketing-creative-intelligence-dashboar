# Irreverent Marketing — Target Audience Personas

## Persona 1: The Frustrated Founder

**Name:** Alex  
**Age:** 28–42  
**Role:** Founder or Co-founder of a DTC or B2B SaaS brand  
**Revenue:** $500k–$5M ARR  

### Profile
Alex has tried multiple agencies and freelancers. They've burned budget on creatives that looked great but didn't convert. They're smart enough to know what bad marketing looks like but don't have the time or team to fix it themselves. They need a system, not another consultant with opinions.

### Pain Points
- Wasted ad spend with nothing to show for it
- Agencies that can't explain why something worked or didn't
- Creative that looks polished but feels off-brand
- No feedback loop between performance data and new creative

### Desires
- Predictable, repeatable creative output
- Clear data on what's working and why
- Speed — less waiting, more shipping
- Feeling like they have a real creative team without the overhead

### Trigger Phrases That Convert
- "Stop guessing. Start running winners."
- "Here's why your last 3 creatives died."
- "What a $10k/day account taught us about ad creative."

---

## Persona 2: The Scaling CMO

**Name:** Jordan  
**Age:** 32–48  
**Role:** CMO or VP Marketing at a growth-stage company  
**Company Size:** 20–200 employees  

### Profile
Jordan has budget and team, but creative output is the bottleneck. They need a system that produces volume without sacrificing quality or brand safety. They're accountable to a board and need to show ROAS improvement quarter over quarter.

### Pain Points
- Creative team can't ship fast enough to keep up with testing needs
- Hard to maintain brand consistency at volume
- No clear process for turning performance data into new creative briefs
- Expensive creative production with inconsistent results

### Desires
- High-volume creative pipeline with quality control baked in
- Clear brief-to-launch workflow the team can follow
- Dashboard visibility into what's winning across campaigns
- A system that scales without proportionally scaling headcount

### Trigger Phrases That Convert
- "Ship 10 variants in the time it takes your team to brief one."
- "Your best-performing creative is already in your data. You just haven't found it yet."
- "Built for teams that need to move faster than their agency."

---

## Persona 3: The Performance Marketer

**Name:** Sam  
**Age:** 25–35  
**Role:** Paid Social Manager or Performance Marketing Lead  
**Context:** In-house at a DTC brand or agency-side  

### Profile
Sam lives in Ads Manager. They understand metrics, know what a good CTR looks like, and can spot a dying creative before the algorithm does. They need better creative inputs — not better targeting advice. They're the internal advocate for a creative system that actually connects to performance data.

### Pain Points
- Creative team doesn't understand what makes an ad perform
- Briefing process is slow and disconnected from what's working
- No systematic way to document and replicate winning hooks
- Testing is ad hoc rather than structured

### Desires
- A brief format that speaks performance marketer language
- Clear hypothesis-driven creative testing frameworks
- Fast turnaround on variant generation when something starts to die
- A single source of truth for what's working across campaigns

### Trigger Phrases That Convert
- "Finally, a creative brief that thinks like a media buyer."
- "Turn your winners into a repeatable playbook."
- "Your CTR tells you what to brief next. Here's how."

## Cross-Persona Themes

| Theme | All Personas |
|-------|-------------|
| **Speed** | Everyone needs faster creative cycles |
| **Data** | Decisions should be driven by performance signals, not opinions |
| **Repeatable Systems** | One-off wins aren't enough — they want a machine |
| **Accountability** | They need to show results to someone (board, boss, themselves) |
