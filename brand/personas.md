# Target Audience Personas — Irreverent Marketing

## Persona 1: The Overwhelmed Operator

**Name:** Marcus  
**Age:** 32–45  
**Role:** E-commerce founder / DTC brand owner  
**Business size:** $500K–$5M ARR  

**What he's dealing with:**
- Running ads himself or with a small team
- Spending money on creative that doesn't convert
- Doesn't have time to analyze — needs answers fast
- Frustrated by agencies that talk a big game but can't show results

**What he wants:**
- A clear system that tells him what's working
- Creatives that actually convert, not just "look good"
- To stop wasting ad budget on guessing games
- More signal, less noise

**How to speak to Marcus:**
- Direct, no BS
- Lead with the problem he recognizes immediately
- Show the solution quickly — he doesn't have time for buildup
- Use numbers and proof points
- CTA: "See how it works" / "Get the system"

**Channels:** Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, YouTube pre-roll

## Persona 2: The Ambitious Growth Marketer

**Name:** Priya  
**Age:** 26–35  
**Role:** Marketing manager or growth lead at a funded startup  
**Business size:** Series A/B, $5M–$50M ARR  

**What she's dealing with:**
- Reporting to a CMO who wants ROAS and CAC metrics
- Testing creative at scale but struggling to identify winning patterns
- Needs to justify ad spend with hard data
- Wants to move faster than the agency can

**What she wants:**
- Creative intelligence that surfaces patterns, not just dashboards
- A system she can hand off to junior team members
- To look smart in front of leadership with clear results
- Speed and repeatability

**How to speak to Priya:**
- Professional but not stiff
- Frame around outcomes and metrics (ROAS, CTR, CAC)
- Respect her intelligence — she knows the space
- CTA: "Book a demo" / "See the dashboard"

**Channels:** LinkedIn, Instagram, Google Display

## Persona 3: The Solo Creator-Turned-Advertiser

**Name:** Jordan  
**Age:** 22–30  
**Role:** Content creator or personal brand monetizing via ads  
**Business size:** Pre-revenue to $500K ARR  

**What they're dealing with:**
- Just starting to run paid ads for the first time
- Making content is easy — performance data is confusing
- Small budget, high stakes
- Doesn't trust agencies (can't afford them anyway)

**What they want:**
- Simple, clear guidance on what to create and why
- To feel like they have a smart co-pilot in their corner
- Quick wins they can share / screenshot
- Affordable or self-serve access

**How to speak to Jordan:**
- Casual, energetic, relatable
- Use social proof from people like them
- Short sentences, mobile-first
- CTA: "Start free" / "Try it yourself"

**Channels:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
